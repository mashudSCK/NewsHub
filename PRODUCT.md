# NewsHub

## Platform

Web; responsive Next.js news reader.

## Purpose

Portfolio project demonstrating editorial design, reliable data handling, and accessible interactions.

## Readers and workflows

Explore headlines and categories, search topics, and save stories locally for later reading.

## Constraints

No accounts, paid-service purchase, AI summaries, database, or email delivery in this release. Preserve the existing Next.js and Tailwind foundation. Public demo content must be labeled. Live provider eligibility and credentials remain deployment prerequisites.
